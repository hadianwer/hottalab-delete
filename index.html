HADI Delivery – Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Since the app does not yet have a built-in deletion feature, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

Email: hadi.salahat@gmail.com

Include the following information in your email:

  • Your registered phone number or email
  • A request to delete your account and associated data

Data that will be deleted:

  • Personal information (name, phone number, email)
  • Order history and delivery addresses

Data that may be retained for legal or compliance reasons (logs) will be deleted within 90 days.
